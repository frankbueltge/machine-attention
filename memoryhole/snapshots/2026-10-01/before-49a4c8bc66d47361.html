bp Ultimate Unleaded with ACTIVE technology

Our best fuel for your Petrol engine

Dirt can form and build up over time on critical parts in petrol engines, such as the fuel injectors and intake valves, and can contribute to:


- Sluggish engine performance
- Increased fuel consumption  

- Less smooth running
- Critical engine parts requiring repair

 

A build-up of dirt can create more dirt within the engine – it’s a vicious cycle

 

bp Ultimate Unleaded with ACTIVE technology is our best fuel to clean and help maintain petrol engines. It starts working from your first fill-up, removing dirt from inside your engine and helping to helping to prevent it from coming back.

 

With ongoing use, bp Ultimate Unleaded with ACTIVE technology helps keep your engine performing as the manufacturer intended and helps reduce the risk of unplanned maintenance.*

 

What's more, it helps keep your vehicle running efficiently - choose bp Ultimate Unleaded with ACTIVE technology for up to 375 more miles per year.**

 

* Benefits achieved with use over time and may vary due to factors including vehicle type and condition, driving style and driving conditions.
**Claim is based on continuous use over 6400 miles per year (UK DFT). For Ultimate Unleaded compared to 95 octane petrol. For Ultimate Diesel based on engines tested in dirty vs. cleaned condition. Benefits may vary due to factors including vehicle, driving style and driving conditions.

ACTIVE technology
bp Ultimate Unleaded with ACTIVE technology contains millions of dirt-busting molecules, specially designed to clean your petrol engine and protect it from harmful dirt. 

 

bp’s innovative formulation fights dirt in two clever ways:

1. ACTIVE molecules attach to existing dirt and pull it away from critical engine parts. The dirt mixes with the fuel and is then safely burnt in the engine.
2. ACTIVE molecules also attach to clean engine surfaces, forming a protective barrier which helps stop dirt adhering to the metal.


 bp Ultimate Unleaded with ACTIVE technology removes existing dirt and forms a barrier against dirt build-up on critical engine parts which, with ongoing use, helps keep your engine performing as the manufacturer intended.* 

 

* Benefits achieved with use over time and may vary due to factors including vehicle type and condition, driving style and driving conditions.
How we did it

Engine technologies evolve and continue to place greater demands on fuels. At bp we work to anticipate and understand these changes, to ensure that our fuel formulations complement the latest engine innovations while still offering benefits to older engines.  

 

bp Ultimate fuels with ACTIVE technology are special. Developed over five years,  our experts subjected them to more than 80 different test methods and tested them for thousands of hours in engines and vehicles to prove the benefits. The result is our best fuel for your petrol or diesel engine.
