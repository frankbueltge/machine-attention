Volkswagen Group ESG Conference 2026

We are pleased to inform you that our 8th ESG Conference for Investors and Analysts took place virtually on 10 June from 15:00–17:30 CEST.

The conference was hosted by Rolf Woller (Head of Group Treasury & Investor Relations) and covered the following topics:

  • Volkswagen Group Sustainability Strategy “regenerate+” – Dr. Dirk Voeste (Chief Sustainability Officer)
  • Group Circular Economy Strategy – Guido Eickenroth (Head of Group Sustainability Strategy & Decarbonisation)
  • Circular Economy: From Strategy to Implementation – Andreas Walingen (Head of Volkswagen Group Circular Economy)
  • Status of Green Finance and recent developments – Rolf Woller (Head of Group Treasury & Investor Relations)

The presentations were followed by Q&A sessions, providing participants with the opportunity to engage with our experts.

A replay will be available shortly:
https://virtualevents.movingimage.com/v/vw_esg_conference_2026

For any questions, please contact the team for Credit & ESG Rating Relations:


Ulrich Hauswaldt (ulrich.hauswaldt@volkswagen.de)
Thomas Küter (thomas.kueter@volkswagen.de)
