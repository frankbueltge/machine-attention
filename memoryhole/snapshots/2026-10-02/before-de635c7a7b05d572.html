One billion barrels: Guyana's remarkable journey continues
  • ExxonMobil and its co-venturers have produced the 1 billionth barrel of oil from Guyana’s Stabroek block, less than seven years after first production began in December 2019.

  • First cohort of 60 pre-service educators will receive specialized mathematics and science training through the Guyana STELLAR Program.
