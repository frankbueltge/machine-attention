Future-proof insulation
How clay improves the energy efficiency of buildings
Clay is one of the oldest building materials in the world – and more relevant than ever. With Cavipor®, a mineral based clay foam, BASF combines traditional materials with modern technology. The result is a sustainable, safe, and efficient insulation solution specifically developed for the energy efficient renovation of existing buildings with cavity walls.
